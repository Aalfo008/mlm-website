About MLM

Muslim Leadership Media

A media organization founded on April 28th, 2023 in Virginia, and a registered nonprofit since December 2023.

Who we are

An alternative platform for our community.

We are committed to creating an alternative entertainment platform for the Muslim community and fostering positive change in the representation of Muslim narratives.

Through inclusive storytelling and media engagement, we also aim to build bridges between diverse faiths and communities — promoting mutual understanding, respect and shared values across cultural and religious boundaries.

In a world saturated with visual pollution and the promotion of values that do not align with our Islamic principles, Muslim Leadership Media aims to be a beacon of light.
Our mission

An alternative to the current offerings in global entertainment.

A values-driven network

Establish a global media network rooted in Islamic values that addresses the pressing need for clean and meaningful content in today's chaotic entertainment industry.

Reviving our heritage

Reviving the stories of our Islamic heritage and the leaders who molded our civilization.

Role models for youth

Offering our youth inspiring role models from our rich civilization to look up to.

Why Muslim voices matter

In today's world, media plays a significant role in shaping perceptions and influencing societal attitudes. However, the representation of Muslims in mainstream media often lacks authenticity and nuance, perpetuating stereotypes and misconceptions.

It is crucial for us to reclaim our narrative and ensure that Muslim voices are heard, valued and accurately represented.

Our organization aims to bridge this gap by nurturing and supporting talented Muslim individuals who aspire to excel in the media industry.

  • Resources and training through the MLM Academy
  • Mentorship from experienced practitioners
  • Networking opportunities to navigate the industry's challenges
  • Original productions that reflect real Muslim experiences
