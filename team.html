Who are we?

Zeyneb & Ahmed

A couple united not just in life but in a profound mission — passionate about reviving the glory of Muslim culture and identity through the powerful medium of media.

Zeyneb, with her filmmaking passion, brings stories to life, aiming to illuminate the richness of Muslim heritage with every scene she crafts. Ahmed, with a background in communications and media, strategizes to ensure these stories reach far and wide, hoping to inspire a new generation of leaders who are proud of their Muslim identity.

Together, they stand as the pillars of an organization dedicated to a narrative that uplifts, educates and empowers — making Muslim Leadership Media more than an organization: a movement towards a Muslim identity renaissance and unity.

Zeyneb and Ahmed live in Virginia, USA, with their two daughters, Anya and Laya.
