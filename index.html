About MLM

Reclaiming our narrative — and building bridges.

Muslim Leadership Media (MLM) is a nonprofit founded on April 28th, 2023 in Virginia.

We are committed to creating an alternative entertainment platform for the Muslim community and fostering positive change in the representation of Muslim narratives. MLM also seeks to build bridges between diverse faiths and communities — through inclusive storytelling and media engagement, we promote mutual understanding, respect and shared values across cultural and religious boundaries.

Initiatives & Programs

Three pillars that turn our mission into action — from the classroom to the camera.

Media Education & Training

Workshops, seminars and training sessions that enhance media literacy, develop critical thinking, and equip young people with the tools to engage effectively in media careers.

Mentorship & Guidance

We connect aspiring Muslim creators with experienced mentors who offer resources, guidance and networking opportunities to help them navigate the industry and succeed.

Media Production

Diverse, engaging content that reflects the true experiences and aspirations of Muslims — films, documentaries, podcasts and digital media.

Our mission

Clean, meaningful content for a chaotic media world.

In a world saturated with visual pollution and values that don't align with our principles, we offer an alternative.

  • Establish a global media network rooted in Islamic values, answering the need for clean and meaningful content.
  • Revive the stories of our Islamic heritage and the leaders who molded our civilization.
  • Offer our youth inspiring role models from our rich civilization to look up to.
  • Nurture talented Muslims who aspire to excel in the media industry.
